What's it worth, in the sense people usually mean

The same short question gets used for face value at a till, the melt of a precious metal, a current asking price, and a formal appraisal. Mixing those jobs is how you feel sure and still get it wrong. A circulating coin can be face value and, separately, have a collector range if the date or mark is scarce. A piece of gold or silver can have a metal floor that isn't the same as a collector or jewellery payout. An insurance document is a further job again.

For most household decisions, what's it worth means: what are similar pieces listed for right now? That's the picture the scanner is built to show.

Photograph the item, with up to three shots if you can add a back, a mark, or a label. Confirm or edit the identification before any price lookup.
